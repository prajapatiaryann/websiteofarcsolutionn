Hard-Won Lessons

WHAT I GOT
WRONG

Every scar here saved a future client's money.

FAILURE 01

What I Tried

Sold a client a full automation suite before understanding their actual workflow.

What Failed

Half the automations ran perfectly — for a process they had already abandoned. 3 weeks of build time, mostly wasted.

What I Learned

Discovery before delivery. I now spend the first week mapping, not building.

FAILURE 02

What I Tried

Wrote content in a "professional" tone because I thought that's what LinkedIn wanted.

What Failed

Zero engagement. The client's audience didn't connect. We looked like every other faceless agency.

What I Learned

Voice first, platform second. Authenticity travels further than polish.

FAILURE 03

What I Tried

Said yes to a rush video project without a brief.

What Failed

Four revision rounds, one unhappy client, and two lost weekends.

What I Learned

No brief, no booking. The intake process is non-negotiable now.
